title: Reference: on-call handoff checklist
---
At the end of a shift hand over open incidents with their current status, anything silenced and until when, known flaky alerts, ongoing maintenance windows and approved load tests, pending follow-ups, and a reminder of who to escalate to. The incoming engineer acknowledges the pager test and reads the incident channel history for the last twelve hours before taking over.
