title: Reference: severity levels SEV1 to SEV4 and what each requires
---
SEV1: complete outage or data loss affecting most customers; page the incident commander and executive on-call, communicate every thirty minutes, human approval is required for risky remediation. SEV2: major feature unavailable or severe degradation for a large group; page the owning team. SEV3: limited impact or a workaround exists; handle in business hours. SEV4: cosmetic or internal-only issues. Severity is assigned on customer impact, not on how alarming the metric looks, and can be raised or lowered as facts arrive.
